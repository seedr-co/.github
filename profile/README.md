---

The best co-founder matches don't happen on job boards. They happen in DMs, group chats and alumni networks, and most builders never get in. Seedr opens that layer to anyone who is shipping. It reads what you've built, finds the founders and ventures that fit, and drafts your first message. You press send.

### Four moves

| | |
|---|---|
| **Card** | Drop in a GitHub username, a CV or LinkedIn's own PDF. Seedr drafts your card from it, and you correct it. No forms. |
| **Drop** | Every morning at 8: up to five people, startups and ideas that fit you, each with the reason. Never padded. |
| **Spark** | A first message, drafted from both cards. Accepted, it opens a chat. |
| **Ideas** | Post a rough idea and who you need. Builders tap "I'd build this", and the ideas that catch become startups. |

### What it won't do

- **Send anything for you.** Every Spark waits for your press.
- **Invent facts.** Drafts use only what's on the two profiles.
- **Fake a crowd.** No seeded members, activity or counts.
- **Charge for the core.** Your card, the Drop, Sparks and messages are free, and stay free.

### How we got here

Seedr started as equity crowdfunding. Before building further, we stress-tested it against SEBI regulations and India's IT Act. As designed, it had no legal path to launch. So we rebuilt it as a discovery layer where no money changes hands. The idea survived; the blocker didn't.

### Under the hood

Next.js and React · Supabase, with row-level security on every table · AI drafts rate-limited per member · PostHog · Sentry · Vercel
